## Background

I have a PhD from The University of Texas at Austin and a portfolio in Applied Statistical Modeling. My work spans language-model systems, machine learning, optimization, robotics, dynamic systems, control, and hybrid physics-based and data-driven modeling.

I am an inventor on 8 US patents and an author of 32+ publications. I also served for more than 10 years as an Associate Editor, overseeing peer review and editorial decisions for more than 250 scientific manuscripts.

Outside of technical work, I grew up on 1990s and early 2000s PC games and still go back to them. Some things just hold up.
